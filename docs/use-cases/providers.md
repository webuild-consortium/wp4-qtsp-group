# Providers

Providers that deliver qualified trust services within WE BUILD, whether
or not they hold a qualified status, and their involvement in the QTSP
group and the use cases. See the [legend and update instructions](README.md).

The *QTSP group* column is based on an entry in the
[QTSP catalogue](https://webuild-consortium.github.io/wp4-qtsp-group/).
Providers without an entry are marked *not confirmed*; the group lead
confirms membership. The *Qualification* column records whether the
provider is qualified for the listed services on the
[EU trusted lists](https://eidas.ec.europa.eu/efda/trust-services/browse/eidas/tls).

> [!NOTE]
> This overview is based on the use case scenario specifications from
> April 2026 and may be outdated. If the status or the parties involved in
> your use case have changed, please update the table, see
> [how to update](README.md#how-to-update).
